THE WORKFLOW

One report.
Three perspectives.

Everyone has a clear role, with the context they need to move the investigation forward.

01

REPORTER

Make the problem clear.

Submit a programming-related bug report with the details needed to reproduce it. Ask AI for clarification questions before submitting.

↗
02

DEVELOPER

Investigate with direction.

Work through assigned reports, keep investigation notes, and review AI-generated checklist suggestions before sharing.

↗
03

MAINTAINER

Keep the queue moving.

Prioritize and assign reports, review proposed resolutions, and use AI summaries to understand the open queue.

↗

WHAT BugBusters PROVIDES

Less guesswork.
